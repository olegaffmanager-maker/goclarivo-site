Frequently Asked Questions

General information — always check the rules that apply in your own country, as regulations vary and can change.

Is online gambling legal where I live? +

That depends entirely on your country — and sometimes your state or region within it. Rules for online gambling vary widely around the world, from clearly licensed and regulated markets to places where it isn't permitted at all. Always check the specific rules where you live before registering with any operator, and only use operators licensed to serve your jurisdiction.

How do I choose the right casino? +

Look at licensing, payout speed, transparent bonus terms and available payment methods — not just the size of the welcome bonus.

What does RTP mean? +

RTP (Return to Player) indicates what percentage of a game's wagers is statistically returned to players over the long run. A higher RTP means better odds over time.

What should I check in a welcome bonus? +

More important than the bonus amount are the wagering requirements (how many times you need to play through the bonus), its validity period, and any restrictions on specific games. This information is in each operator's terms and conditions.

How do I protect myself from risky gambling behavior? +

Reputable operators let you set deposit, loss and time limits, and offer self-exclusion tools — use them before you start playing, not after. Organizations such as Gamblers Anonymous offer support and meeting information in many countries, and directories such as Find A Helpline can point you to a local service.
